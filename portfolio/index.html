Why it exists

Answers are cheap.
Evidence is the product.

Most research demos stop at a fluent paragraph. Sourcebound stops at the paragraph only after it can show which sources arrived, what each source said, and where confidence should stay modest.

Plan before prose

Question decomposition, query caps, freshness signals, and an explicit stop decision bound retrieval.

Keep source ownership

The application owns URLs, IDs, snippets, offsets, and provider health. The model owns synthesis.

Verify the claims

A deterministic lexical pass exposes matched passages, unresolved citations, and support gaps.
